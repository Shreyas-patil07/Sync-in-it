# Related Products — Not Equivalent to Synchronized Audio

**Catalogue IDs:** 37–38 · **Research date:** 7 November 2026

## 37 — PlayOver

- **Workflow:** Cross-platform music-link converter that maps a music link to matching links in other music services.
- **Synchronization boundary:** None. It does not synchronize playback or distribute audio.
- **Why included:** Useful adjacent discovery/link-transfer product, but not a timing competitor.
- **Unknowns:** Matching accuracy, supported service coverage and availability can change.
- **Source:** [Official site](https://playover.app/)

## 38 — JukeboxDuo

- **Workflow:** Browser-based collaborative jukebox for desktop/mobile browsers.
- **Synchronization boundary:** The site claims a shared room clock and synchronized playback and describes a Next.js + WebSocket stack; exact drift algorithm and independent performance are not established in this research.
- **Why included:** Adjacent reference for browser-based collaborative queue/room UX, not a proven raw audio distribution engine.
- **Unknowns:** Clock model, playback correction, state authority, reconnect, queue conflict handling and acoustic results.
- **REA focus:** Inspect public product behaviour, simultaneous queue changes, late join, disconnect/rejoin and cross-browser timing claims.
- **Sources:** [Features](https://jukeboxduo.com/features), [about](https://jukeboxduo.com/about), [site](https://jukeboxduo.com/)

## Classification warning

Do not count link conversion, collaborative queue management or a shared room UI as proof of synchronized multi-device audio. Identify exactly what is synchronized—track identity, playhead, digital samples or physical acoustic output—before comparing systems.
